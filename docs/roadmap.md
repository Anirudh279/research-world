# Roadmap: confidence before scale

Research World is a development alpha. These are directions, not shipped claims or delivery promises.

1. **Make first use reproducible.** Independent clean Linux/macOS sessions; actionable setup failures; verify hosted provider contracts live under explicit budgets.
2. **Make evidence quality measurable.** More redistributable extraction failures, temporal tests, complete observation records and independent semantic review.
3. **Make experiments portable.** More agent examples, documented comparison protocols and community-authored frozen challenges.
4. **Earn broader benchmark claims.** Preregistered multi-question studies, independent replications and clear separation of collection quality from reasoning quality.

A leaderboard comes after a defensible evaluation protocol. More captured pages alone do not establish either.
