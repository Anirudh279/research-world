Research World examples

mossbank/: A portable fictional bulletin plus a real Codex/Claude comparison.
Open its standalone report, verify the frozen bytes and recompute the saved
metrics, then run your own agent against that snapshot. No keys are needed to
inspect or verify the example; fresh LLM runs need your chosen account.

For broader synthetic tasks, install the river, energy or civic world from the
dashboard. Each has three time slices and an explicit missing source. See
../docs/starters.txt. For a new live question, follow ../docs/first-session.txt.

Examples are demonstrations, not hidden evaluator material or model leaderboards.
Do not give an agent these completed reports when measuring its research ability.
